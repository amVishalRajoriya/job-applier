5. LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukri.com

Read this first. These three sites forbid scraping and automated use in their terms, sit behind logins, and use CAPTCHAs and bot detection. Accounts that look automated get restricted or banned. So the agent never logs in to them, never searches or crawls them, and never fills or submits their forms. Instead it reads what you already have: the job-alert emails they send you, and job pages you save from your own browser. Applying stays with you: the agent prepares a kit (tailored resume, cover letter, answers from your profile), you submit on the site, then mark it as submitted. These readers were built and tested on synthetic copies of the pages and emails (the sites cannot be reached from where this was built), so treat the first results with care.

a. Job-alert emails (recommended). Turn on job alerts on each site. Then either save the alert emails as .eml files (or an .mbox export) into this folder:

or let the agent read your mailbox read-only over IMAP (it only looks at mail from linkedin.com, indeed.com and naukri.com, and never marks, moves or deletes anything). Use an app password, put it in an environment variable, and type that variable's name here, never the password.

b. Saved job pages. Open a job on the site in your own browser (signed in), press Ctrl+S / Cmd+S and save it into this folder. Alert emails only carry a summary, so saving the page gives a full description and a better score.

c. Paste job URLs to keep a to-do list of pages to save, one per line. Nothing is fetched.
